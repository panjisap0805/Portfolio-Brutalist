Why this even started.

The helpdesk was drowning in one question: "where is my loan?" Applicants had zero visibility into status, and the application and disbursement process ran long. Before touching a single screen, I audited the existing app through UX laws and heuristics — the findings reshaped what the research actually needed to answer.
